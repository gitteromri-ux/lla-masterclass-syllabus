The Syllabus

Sixty minutes, five parts

Every minute earns its place. The class is structured so that a total newcomer leaves with a clear operating system, and an existing longevity-curious viewer leaves knowing what actually matters and what does not.

Part 1 · The setup — 5 minutes

Who Julie is, and why this hour is different. The class opens with Julie's DunedinPACE score of 0.665 and her #2 Rejuvenation Olympics ranking (past tense — the correct framing for reporters), on a ~$12-a-day budget. The one-sentence promise of the class: learn to age slower from the second slowest-aging human on earth.

  • The person — a single mom, working full-time, who outperformed most of the billionaire biohacker class.
  • The promise — a repeatable operating system, not a lecture.
  • The frame — the class is the appetizer; the Blueprint is the full meal.

Part 2 · Debunking longevity — 10 minutes

Separating what matters from what does not. Longevity is loud right now, and most of the noise wastes people's money and time. Julie walks through the current landscape and cuts it down to what is actually supported.

  • The billionaire trap — the $2M/year Bryan Johnson model is not the point of reference; consistency is.
  • What doesn't move the needle — exotic supplements, wearable overload, extreme protocols people cannot sustain.
  • What actually does — the boring inputs, applied every day, that show up in the biomarkers.
  • Why $12 a day beat $2M — the honest answer, in plain language.

Part 3 · The three pillars — 30 minutes

The core teaching hour. Ten minutes on each pillar — the science first in one clear sentence, then the daily behaviour, then the specific numbers Julie hits.

Pillar 1 · Sleep — 10 min

  • Why it matters — sleep is the recovery layer everything else runs on.
  • The rules — lights out at 9pm, up at 5am, dinner three hours before bed, no screens in the last hour.
  • The trap — the wearable-obsession loop and how to avoid it.
  • The takeaway — one habit to change tomorrow morning.

Pillar 2 · Food — 10 min

  • Why it matters — the single biggest lever most people ignore.
  • The rules — a pound of vegetables a day, protein targets by bodyweight, meal timing, coffee timing.
  • The trap — supplement culture as a substitute for eating properly.
  • The takeaway — one plate rule that fixes 80% of the mistakes.

Pillar 3 · Movement — 10 min

  • Why it matters — strength, VO2-max, and daily movement together drive healthspan.
  • The rules — the weekly training pattern Julie uses (strength, zone-2, sprint intervals), plus steps.
  • The trap — the cardio-only, or strength-only, dead ends.
  • The takeaway — one workout structure anyone can start on Monday.

Part 4 · Her protocol, in one page — 10 minutes

The whole day, hour by hour. Julie walks through what an actual day looks like: wake time, morning protocol, meals, training window, evening wind-down, sleep. Includes the ~$100-a-month supplement stack, dose by dose, so viewers can copy or adapt it directly.

  • The day — every hour, in one clean walkthrough.
  • The stack — what she takes, how much, when.
  • The budget — how it stays inside $12 a day of health spend.

Part 5 · The next step — 5 minutes

The bridge into the Longevity Blueprint. A warm invitation, not a hard sell — the one-hour class is the framework, the Blueprint is where each person builds their own tailored version with the LLA faculty.

  • What the Blueprint adds — tailored plans, faculty support, longer arc, deeper science.
  • What attendees walk away with — a clear picture of what to do tomorrow, and where to go if they want the full thing.
