The 17 slides, her headlines verbatim, her on-screen direction
Welcome. I am very normal. Navy, lit. Build: the two facts huge, "Second in the world, 2023" and "About twelve dollars a day." Official Longevity Masterclass title card first, matching the website; she talks on camera.
Why I went down this path. One photograph full bleed, headline low and left. (No photos of Julie in the deck per the later rule, so the two ages carry it: 70 still carrying the groceries, 90 still holding a conversation.)
Ten years pass. By this test, I age about six. The speed towards death. The plain line first. Then the gauge, hero size, 1.0 at the top, needle on 0.65, ticks at 0.665 and 0.76. Approved visual.
Longevity is like a cake. One root, four branches (aging; heart disease, cancer, dementia, type 2 diabetes). Then the cake, hero, whole and uncut, every layer lit: Sleep, Food, Movement, Stress Management, the thin layer, the icing labelled Therapies. Approved visual. Returns at each layer, lit.
Not wrong. Not first. The icing lit, then the cost map: cost left to right, human evidence bottom to top; sauna, prebiotics and most supplements, NAD drips, peptides, stem cells, rapamycin with a doctor, the two-million program off the chart. Four turn amber. A coin: twelve dollars a day.
Menopause therapy is a layer, not icing. The thin layer lit. The window, menopause to ten years after. Then the four layers light and the headline becomes: Four layers hold up the cake.
Sleep is the repair shift. The cake, sleep lit. Then the night, 9 PM to 5 AM, the five numbers only: lights out 9, up 5, last meal three hours before bed, screens off one hour before, an eight-hour opportunity, cool and dark.
"I'll sleep when I die," said the person aging at twice the normal speed. Nothing but the line.
Take the religion out of your diet. The cake, food lit. The plate from above, three parts: protein the floor, fiber the lever, whole food the default. Five numbers: 1 lb vegetables, 1 g protein per pound of goal weight, window 8 to 6, coffee cutoff 10 AM, zero alcohol on training days. Fiber 25 to 38 g; 90% of women and 97% of men miss it.
The one thing every diet agrees on. The sugar slide, Omri's build, three panels on one object: Spike (glucose curve), Signal (insulin and nutrient sensing on, a hallmark of aging), Scar (glycation, HbA1c). Then two checkboxes: Move dinner earlier. Rebuild one plate. Your two Monday changes.
Same age. The cake, movement lit. Two people full height, same age, same light, one stands tall, one leans on a cane; two words, cream, centered low. Under each a bar, years lived and years lived well, the gap 12.4 years. (Later rule: no generated people, so this becomes the healthspan model.)
Four types of movement, four parts of you. Body map, four points lighting: bones and muscle, heart, heart, brain. Weights (grip strength), VO2 max (fitness), Zone 2 (7,000 steps), mobility and balance (one-leg stand), each with its predictor.
What my week looks like. Seven blocks Monday to Sunday coloured by type; step floor under all seven. (Headline rule: it must read as her weekly longevity protocol, not "what my week looks like".)
The layer people skip. The cake, stress lit, no graph. Then the breath: two in through the nose, one long out through the mouth.
The day is built backward from bedtime. The day ring, 5 AM to 9 PM, 9 PM at the top, every hour coloured by its layer, the eating window 8 to 6 as one band. Headline on the slide: Julie Gibson Clark's Longevity Protocol. The gold takeaway, the place for the big 3D design; people screenshot it.
Test. Then buy. A notebook: "You don't need a lab. You need a notebook." Three tests: Vitamin D, Homocysteine, Red blood cell nutrient levels. Then her list with monthly costs, one total, NOVOS disclosed, under "What one person chose."
Why. Intention. Resources. The closing white cake, sliced. The ring of three, the centre lights only when all three are on. Blueprint card: "The same cake, built week by week, with a teacher." Your why, your intention, your resources, one thing, forty-eight hours.
Content notes from the Oct 15 call (Omri's)
Opening: her story and her why before the heavy material. Power sentences: "Longevity is like a cake", "The speed towards death." Icing, not core: NAD, stem cells, sauna, prebiotics, most supplements, peptides. Menopause therapy is its own thin layer, not icing; find a doctor through the Menopause Society. Sleep hygiene: last meal 3 hours before bed, strict bedtime, routine, 8-hour opportunity. Nutrition: not the type of diet; take the religion out; the one thing everyone agrees on is sugar. Sugar gets its own slide. Movement: half the screen each, the person with a cane left, the active person right with callouts on the body for the four exercise types (brain, heart, bones, feet). Imagery: real longevity science a professor would show, scans, renders, real models with true sources; every slide answers what the audience wants to know; clear is not simplistic.
Design rules (Omri's)
Playfair Display italic headlines, large; white, blue, cream or black text only; no bold; no pills, chips, bordered card grids, template symmetry; no em dashes or middots; alternate light and dark folds, a bright version must exist. Image first; the six-layer cake a true 3D hero, whole and uncut, only the closing white cake sliced; site footage must actually move, no stills cut from it; no text over imagery; nothing sci-fi; no generated people at all; no old-looking women, no intimidating bodybuilder exercise; no blood imagery on the testing slide; real sourced models, McKinsey-style; dense charts forbidden. Type doubled: nothing small anywhere, every graphic surrounded by large text; source lines 36 px and up. Phone-first legibility. Every round on one open GitHub link with all rounds in order, newest labelled last version; a redo is a new link, never overwrite. Time estimates while working; no technical talk; no pop-ups; no files in chat. Before designing, read the document and build each slide around its one powerful meaning; be able to explain truthfully why each design fits its moment.
