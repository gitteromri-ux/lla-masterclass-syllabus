WHY I WENT DOWN THIS PATH

Not for a leaderboard.
For my son.

Not just alive. Here. Strong enough to carry the groceries, clear enough to be useful, well enough to show up for the parts of his life I haven't seen yet.

When you watch what aging takes from someone you love, you stop wondering whether. You start asking how fast. I am an engineer. I wanted a number on it.

Measure.
Don't wonder.
The one rule behind everything tonight.

09 / 17
SUGAR AND AGING

The one thing every diet agrees on.

Spike
The curve after sugar or simple carbs. What a glucose monitor shows.
Signal
Insulin and the nutrient-sensing pathways switch on and stay on. Deregulated nutrient sensing is a hallmark of aging.
Scar
Sugar bonds to proteins: glycation. Stiff arteries, wrinkles. HbA1c is the measured version.

Chiu, Epel and Laraia, UCSF, JAMA Network Open 2024: each gram of added sugar, an older biological age. Huang et al., BMJ 2023: 45 of 83 outcomes harmed. López-Otín et al., Cell 2023.
