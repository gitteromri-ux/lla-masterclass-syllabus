That's what my week looks like. And I know what some of you are thinking: how on earth am I supposed to fit all of this into my life? Please remember: I didn't build this schedule in a day. This is twenty-five years of adding, subtracting and fine-tuning what works for me. When I'm traveling or under tight deadlines, it looks different. But the core bones are always there. This is just an example of how a diversified portfolio can look, not a test you have to pass tomorrow. If you're wondering where to begin, start small. Recent research shows that taking short movement breaks, like a few sets of bodyweight air squats spaced throughout your day, can drop your post-meal blood sugar spikes by over twenty percent. Contracting those big leg muscles pulls glucose straight out of your blood, making mini-breaks like that twice as effective at managing blood sugar as a single thirty-minute workout and sitting for the rest of the day. So if this all feels like too much right now, take a breath. A daily walk and a few air squats while your coffee brews is a very respectable place to start. And for those of you who know you need to check in with your doctor before starting a new routine, please do. The goal isn't perfection on day one. The goal is simply to start moving.
