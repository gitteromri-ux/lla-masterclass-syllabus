Wilmanski, Nature Metabolism 2021

Now for food. Before we get into details, I want to take the type of diet completely off the table. Vegan, keto, Mediterranean, carnivore: it's not about the name. I've done vegan, I've done keto, I call them try-its now. We need to take the religion out of our diet and stop arguing over how virtuous you are because you can stick to a set of dietary commandments. Let's just look at what to put on your plate through the lens of what longevity researchers have figured out so far. I see four universal pillars. One, fiber variety: not just enough fiber, variety. A highly diverse microbiome is one of the clearest biological signatures of healthy aging; different plant fibers and polyphenols act as unique fuels that quiet inflammation, regulate metabolic health, support immune balance and protect your brain. Two, protein adequacy: as we age, losing muscle is one of the biggest threats to independence. Muscle is the physical representation of your longevity savings account. With anabolic resistance, we need more high-quality protein in a single meal to trigger the same muscle-building signal we got effortlessly in our twenties. Three, polyphenol and micronutrient density: eat the rainbow. Deeply colored vegetables, dark berries, leafy greens, extra virgin olive oil, green tea, even coffee. They trigger your master antioxidant defenses, feed key gut bacteria, keep your vascular system flexible. Four, minimal ultra-processed foods: real whole foods over industrial combinations of refined grains, added sugar and questionable oils. Studies tracking tens of thousands of people show a direct line between high ultra-processed intake and mortality. Here is how it looks in my real life: about a pound of vegetables a day, half of it greens, varied week to week; wild blueberries or pomegranate seeds nearly every day; a gram of protein per pound of lean muscle mass, focusing on what I'm adding, not what I'm missing; a ten-hour eating window, green tea, sometimes coffee. It took years. Please start slowly; your gut will make you regret an overnight pound of vegetables. If you have a condition where dietary shifts affect medications, coordinate with your doctor.
