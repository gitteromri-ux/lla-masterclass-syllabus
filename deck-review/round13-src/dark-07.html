I'll tell you how I like to think of this, especially every time a new therapy comes up on my feed: I think of the Blue Zones, the places on earth where people live longest. And I remind myself: no one there is lying in a hyperbaric chamber or injecting peptides, or even has a gym membership. They're walking, they're gardening, eating well, sleeping and waking with the sun, and they're rooted in their communities. So when I look at that icing stuff, I don't feel left out. I'd advise you to leave a lot of these out of your focus until you have a good handle on sleep, diet, movement and stress management. Not perfected. A good handle. For the record, I do some of these things: a longevity supplement combination, and I love the sauna. Again: not don't. Just not first.
