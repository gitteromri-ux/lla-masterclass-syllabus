People often think tracking your health metrics has to be expensive. It isn't. Almost every layer of the foundation we've talked about tonight shows up in a simple number you can measure without dropping a fortune at a high-end lab. A pencil, a basic notebook, and a modest smartwatch or scale will tell you most of what you need to know: your daily steps, your resting heart rate, your sleep duration, and your blood pressure. The goal isn't to measure every single variable in your life. The goal is simply to stop guessing. Which brings me to supplements. I'm keeping this section brief on purpose, because the best supplement advice I can give you is not a recommended list. My stack is tailored strictly to my body, my genetics and my blood work. Yours should be too. The most powerful step you can take is working with your physician or a functional medicine doctor to see where your baseline actually sits: your vitamin D, your homocysteine, your inflammatory markers, your micronutrient levels. The order of operations matters: test first, then targeted buying. Think of lifestyle as the cake and supplements as the icing. A blood test simply tells you where the cake has a hole, so you know where to put the icing.
