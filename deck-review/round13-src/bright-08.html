Before we bake, one thing that could get filed under icing by mistake, and I used to think of it like that: menopause therapy. For half of this room, menopause will change your sleep, your bones, your mood, your appetite, your energy, even the shape of your body. This period of life changes how well every other layer can do its job. Even with perfect sleep hygiene, it's nearly impossible to sleep well if your hormones are crashing and you're up with hot flashes. The same goes for eating and exercise. If you're doing all the right things but your scale is moving upward, or you're suffering from low energy, please don't think this is simply an eat less and move more issue. This is a thin layer of the cake, at the bottom, before all the other layers. The Menopause Society's own position, updated in 2022, is that for healthy women with symptoms who are under sixty, or within ten years of menopause, the benefits of hormone therapy generally outweigh the risks. That's their sentence, not mine. And in November 2025 the FDA announced it was removing the black-box warnings from menopausal hormone therapy, the warnings that had kept a generation of women and their doctors away from it. The only action from this slide is one line: find a doctor or nurse practitioner who specializes in hormone therapy. I found mine through the Menopause Society. Look for practitioners who passed the competency exam in menopause care, the letters MSCP. Don't start taking hormones because you heard it in a webinar. Have a conversation with a knowledgeable doctor. Now, with the icing made and the hormone layer discussed, let's move to the four main layers: sleep, food, movement, and stress management.
