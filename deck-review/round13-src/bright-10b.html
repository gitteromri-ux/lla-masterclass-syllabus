In fact, wrinkles are the visible proof of this. Advanced Glycation End-products, literally called AGEs, are what happens when sugar cross-links with the collagen and elastin in your skin, making those proteins stiff and caramelized, much like the crispy top of a creme brulee. But if that stiffening is happening on your face, it's likely happening inside your body as well: stiffening your arteries, clouding the lenses of your eyes, wearing down the delicate filters in your kidneys. There's a very elegant test for this: the HbA1c blood test. It measures the percentage of sugar stuck to the protein in your blood, a direct window into glycation before it shows up as permanent damage.
