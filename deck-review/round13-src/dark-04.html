So you know why I became focused on longevity and what test I use. But why did science and the whole world become kind of obsessed? It turns out aging is the biggest risk factor for the four things we're all afraid of: heart disease, cancer, dementia and type 2 diabetes. According to geroscientists, if you slow the aging process itself, you push back all four at once. Geroscience is researchers, scientists and clinicians working out the best way to navigate aging without the decades of decline and dis-ease.
